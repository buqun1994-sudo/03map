<Paragraph=0,0,0,32><TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,230>1. What is a real-world traffic sharing plan?</TextColor></TextSize></Paragraph>

<Paragraph=0,0,0,32><TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,153>After you connect specific smart rearview mirrors, smart driving recorders or other similar devices to the mobile network of the smart car box through the wifi hotspot, you can turn on and use this function, which allows you to share various types with us and other users Road condition information such as real-life pictures of road traffic incidents. For example: in the event of congestion, construction, road closures, traffic accidents, bad weather, etc., while hearing the voice broadcast, users can also view the pictures of the corresponding traffic incidents through amap products and services applicable to various hardware terminals . In addition, this feature allows you to help us improve our products and services, so that you and other users have a better product experience. For example, it can help us improve the comprehensiveness and accuracy of map data and real-time road conditions, and realize smarter map navigation planning. I would like to remind you not to watch and operate this software in a driving vehicle.</TextColor></TextSize></Paragraph>

<Paragraph=0,0,0,32><TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,230>2. What do you need to participate in this plan?</TextColor></TextSize></Paragraph>

<Paragraph=0,0,0,32><TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,153>In the spirit of "all for me, I for all", in order to achieve real-world traffic sharing and improve our products and services, you need to share with us and agree that we collect, use and share your smart products according to this plan Real-life traffic pictures related to traffic incidents, road conditions, and traffic facilities taken by sight glasses, smart driving recorders, or other similar equipment, as well as corresponding location information and related equipment information. This feature consumes network traffic, but we will make reasonable efforts to strictly control the consumption of network traffic.</TextColor></TextSize></Paragraph>

<Paragraph=0,0,0,32><TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,230>3. What will we do to participate in this plan?</TextColor></TextSize></Paragraph>

<Paragraph=0,0,0,32><TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,153>After you participate in the program, we may collect real-life traffic pictures related to traffic incidents, road conditions, and traffic facilities recorded by your smart rearview mirror, smart driving recorder or other similar devices, as well as corresponding location information and related equipment information We may process the collected information accordingly, and may publish the processed real-life traffic pictures to amap products and services applicable to various hardware terminals to help users understand the corresponding intuitively and quickly The road traffic conditions of the location; we may refer to the corresponding real-life traffic pictures to improve our map data and other products and services, so that users can use more comprehensive and accurate products and services more quickly; and, we also The above-mentioned related information will be used in accordance with other provisions of the AutoNavi Privacy Policy. For details on the collection, use and protection of personal information, please refer to the relevant content of the "AutoNavi Privacy Policy".</TextColor></TextSize></Paragraph>

<Paragraph=0,0,0,32><TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,153>You can turn on/off this function in this software's System Settings--Other--Real Scene Traffic Sharing Plan. You understand and agree that participating in or opening this plan means that you have agreed to us in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations to collect, use, and share the above-mentioned related information in accordance with the plan and the AutoNavi Privacy Policy. If you close the program, you may not be able to view the real-life traffic pictures shared by other users; and you can no longer help us improve our products and services, and you will not be able to experience a better product experience faster.</TextColor></TextSize></Paragraph>
