<TextSize=24><TextColor=255,255,255,153><Paragraph=0,0,0,40>The adapted version is the software that has been matched with the car factory and is built into the car at the factory, which is more compatible with the original car. <TextSize=24><TextColor=255,255,255,153><Paragraph=0,0,0,40>After the car mirror is connected to the Internet, enter the login page of the car mirror version of amapauto, open the mobile version of the amap toolbox function, and select [Scan] to quickly log in to the car mirror version of amapauto.
