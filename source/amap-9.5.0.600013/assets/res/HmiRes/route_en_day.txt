<TextSize=28><TextColor=0,0,0,230><Paragraph=0,0,0,16>1. How to add a waypoint?</Paragraph></TextColor></TextSize>
<TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,153><Paragraph=0,0,0,40>After the route is planned successfully, click on the "More" icon and find "Search for Waypoints" at the top of the card to add it. Up to three waypoints can be added.</Paragraph></TextColor></TextSize>
<TextSize=28><TextColor=0,0,0,230><Paragraph=0,0,0,16>2. How to set navigation preferences?</Paragraph></TextColor></TextSize>
<TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,153><Paragraph=0,0,0,40>After the route planning is successful, click Preferences, you can choose to avoid congestion/avoid tolls/no highway, etc. according to your personal situation. Turn on the avoidance restriction to plan an exclusive route that meets the license plate number.</Paragraph></TextColor></TextSize>
<TextSize=28><TextColor=0,0,0,230><Paragraph=0,0,0,16>3. How to search for facilities along the way?</Paragraph></TextColor></TextSize>
<TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,153><Paragraph=0,0,0,40>After the route planning is successful, click "More"-"Search along the way"  and select the type of facilities along the route that need to be searched in the expanded card.</Paragraph></TextColor></TextSize>
<TextSize=28><TextColor=0,0,0,230><Paragraph=0,0,0,16>4. Why is the route planned offline different from the online?</Paragraph></TextColor></TextSize>
<TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,153><Paragraph=0,0,0,40>Online route planning will be combined with real-time traffic data and some preference settings for real-time calculations. Offline route planning will only be calculated based on offline data, and there will also be avoidances that support time-based prohibition.</Paragraph></TextColor></TextSize>
<TextSize=28><TextColor=0,0,0,230><Paragraph=0,0,0,16>5. How to switch between main and side roads?</Paragraph></TextColor></TextSize>
<TextSize=24><TextColor=0,0,0,153><Paragraph=0,0,0,40>The navigation will automatically determine whether there is a main and side road relationship at the location. If it exists, the [Main and Auxiliary Road Switch] button will appear in the lower right corner of the navigation interface. After clicking to switch the main and auxiliary roads, the main and side road positioning will be switched and the route will be retrieved.</Paragraph></TextColor></TextSize>
